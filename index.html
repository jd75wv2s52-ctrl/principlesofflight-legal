How aircraft fly, explained simply

Principles of Flight is an iPhone and iPad course on aerodynamics and flight: 84 short lessons across 14 sections, from the four forces and how a wing makes lift to high-speed flight, stability and aeroelasticity. Every section has clear, zoomable diagrams, real aircraft photos, quick checks and a self-test.
