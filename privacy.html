1. Who we are

This policy explains how Principles of Flight (“we”, “us”) handles personal data in the Principles of Flight app. Contact: apexacademic@proton.me.

2. The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address or any other contact details.
  • Your lesson progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We show no ads, use no analytics, and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We have no servers of our own, so we do not receive any of your data.

3. What data is handled

Subscription data

If you subscribe, Apple takes the payment and gives us no card or payment details. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock subscriber content. This check uses Apple’s own system; no subscription data is sent to us.

Data that stays on your device

Which lessons you have completed, your progress, and whether your subscription is active are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. They are removed if you delete the app.

Technical data

When the app talks to the App Store, Apple may process technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. The app may also download its typefaces from Google Fonts, in which case Google receives your IP address as part of delivering the font file.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or health data, and we do not use advertising identifiers or third-party advertising or analytics tools.

4. Who else handles data

  • Apple – App Store payments and subscriptions (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • Google Fonts – delivery of typefaces (Google Privacy Policy).

These providers act under their own privacy policies. Some process data in the United States and other countries. We do not sell your data.

5. How long data is kept

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Subscription records are kept by Apple under its own policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them, or where the law requires.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data, or object to processing. Because we hold no personal data about you beyond any emails you send us, most requests can be met by deleting the app. You can email us for anything else at apexacademic@proton.me. We will reply within 30 days. Where the law gives you this right, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The app is suitable for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before buying a subscription.

8. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Connections to Apple and Google are encrypted.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; if a change is significant we will tell you in the app.

10. Contact
