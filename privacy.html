Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Short version: AeroLearn does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No account is required, and all data stays on your device.

What information we collect

AeroLearn does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, age, location, or any other identifying information.

The app stores your lesson progress (which lessons you have completed) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use any analytics tools, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or third-party tracking of any kind. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

Third-party services

AeroLearn does not integrate with any third-party services. There are no social login options, payment processors, or external APIs called at runtime.

Data storage

The only data stored by the app is your lesson completion progress, held in your device's local storage. You can clear this data at any time by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

AeroLearn does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, the app does not raise concerns under COPPA or similar laws.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page and release a new version of the app. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
