Common questions

What is free?

In sections 1–3, lessons 1–3 and the section self-test are free; in sections 4–6, lessons 1–2 are free. The flight timeline and glossary are always free. A subscription unlocks everything else.

I subscribed on another device. How do I unlock the app?

Open any locked lesson to show the subscription screen, then tap Restore previous purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions and choose Principles of Flight. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
